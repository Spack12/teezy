Quiet / steady / packed is how many groups the course has sent out in the 90 minutes before that tee time, compared with how many its start interval allows. It's a tee-sheet estimate, not measured pace of play. The ⏱ round time is an estimate built from the same numbers: a normal pace for walking or riding, sped up or slowed down by how full the tee sheet is ahead of you and how far apart the course spaces its tee times.
